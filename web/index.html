What a check-in proves

  1. The pass was issued by an account whose ML-DSA-87 key the chain knows, over a message bound to this contract and chain id.
  2. The presenter holds the ML-DSA-87 key whose hash the pass was issued to — a copied pass id or a screenshot is not enough.
  3. Every issue, check-in and revoke is a transaction: indicative only if the signature verifies in the pqcVerify precompile.
